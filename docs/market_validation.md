# MigrationProof: commercial validation sprint

## Narrow buyer and claim

**Target buyer hypothesis:** Microsoft data migration consultancies responsible for SQL Server to Azure SQL transitions.

**Pitch:** "MigrationProof creates an independent, explainable evidence report that checks whether selected schema, row-count and numeric-aggregate signals match across a migration. It doesn't perform the migration."

Never promise complete equivalence, regulatory certification, automatic remediation or independently verified accuracy.

## Two-week discovery sprint

- Contact 10-15 migration leads and consulting principals; ask for 15-minute interviews.
- Discover how they sign off migrations, what breaks, who owns the sign-off, and whether evidence is currently produced manually.
- Demo with synthetic lab databases only. Show both a clean self-check and a deliberate same-row-count defect.
- Offer a bounded **design-partner pilot**, not a production assurance certificate. Pricing hypothesis: $2,500 fixed-fee for one agreed test migration and a human-reviewed evidence report. Validate willingness to pay before committing to pricing.
- Pilot agreement must specify scope, authorized systems, read-only privileges, retention, data handling, exclusions, deliverables and no certification claims.

## Qualification questions

1. How many SQL Server migrations have you completed in the last year?
2. What evidence is required for sign-off, and who approves it?
3. What is the cost of a failed cutover or delayed acceptance?
4. Which checks are manual or repeated across projects?
5. Would a scoped evidence report justify a paid pilot, and who signs the purchase order?

## Stop/go evidence

Proceed to paid pilots only after an independent source/target lab demonstration, least-privilege and TLS validation, query-cost controls, and explicit pilot scope. Record actual interviews, objections, commitments and conversion; do not infer market demand from demo success.
